# New sources expand urban election evidence

**Bengaluru 2010→2015 adds a fourth urban city; Chandigarh 2011→2016 adds another cycle.** Both now have source-checked ward panels. Bengaluru retains all 198 wards, with 101 later nonquota wards and one unresolved winner outcome in that comparison. Chandigarh has 26 linked wards and complete honorific-based outcomes. Rajasthan offers much larger held returns, but historical ward links remain unresolved. Goa adds actual result gazettes with measurement and geography gaps. The comparison files and source receipts are preserved upstream; the research manuscript computes effect estimates separately.

The comparison remains **prior women-quota assignment versus prior nonquota assignment, among seats not reserved for women at the later election**. Women who won earlier nonquota seats remain in the comparison group. The treatment is assignment to a quota, not exposure to a woman officeholder. A usable comparison requires actual election outcomes, dated prior/current reservations, independently measured winner sex, and documented electoral geography. Source completeness alone does not establish the assignment assumptions needed for causal inference.

## Bengaluru adds a city; Chandigarh adds a cycle

Bengaluru's newly recovered [2010 SEC winner gazette](https://web.archive.org/web/20201117030355id_/https://karsec.gov.in/PreElectionHandler.ashx?id=63&Key=R) and [2015 winner gazette](https://web.archive.org/web/20201102050802id_/https://karsec.gov.in/PreElectionHandler.ashx?id=65&Key=R) contain ward numbers, reservations, elected names, printed titles, addresses, and parties. The [2015 implementing notification](https://web.archive.org/web/20150824072502id_/http://karsec.gov.in/BBMP-2015/reservation.pdf), physical pages 1–2, restores the reservation roster and refers to the July 15, 2009 boundary notification. The retained [2009 boundary schedule](https://praja.in/files/BBMPwards.pdf), physical page 1 and its annexure, defines the 198 single-member wards and their boundaries. This establishes far more than matching ward counts. The final June 22 judicial text remains a located/indexed source rather than a retained original. The files are in [Karnataka raw sources](../../../karnataka/raw/urban_expansion).

The [manual review](../../../karnataka/manual/urban_expansion/bbmp_reservations_review.csv) covers **396 year–ward records**, including caste categories: 66 women-reserved wards in 2010 and 97 in 2015. The transition counts are 25 quota exits, 76 nonreserved in both elections, 41 reserved in both, and 56 newly reserved. The second review corrected the initial transcription of ward 189 in 2010; it is nonquota in the original, but reserved in 2015 and therefore outside the later-open sample. Relative to the secondary CSV, the women-flag errors are wards 5 and 189 in 2010 and ward 4 in 2015. Five 2015 caste labels also differ between winner and pre-election documents; the reinstated pre-election notification takes priority. [Source review and typed panel](../../../karnataka/build/urban_expansion/README.md).

All **198 original 2015 winner titles** have now been reviewed and reconciled against the complete secondary return. Two source conflicts remain missing: ward 112 prints Shrimati versus secondary M, while ward 197 prints Shri versus secondary F. Ward 197 is currently reserved and outside the comparison. Ward 132 prints Dr.; its title-only value remains missing, while explicit secondary M is corroborated for the same person and ward by Shri Dr S Raju in the retained ward-budget brief, physical page 263. The reconciled outcome is kept separate from each source. Among eligible wards, the previously reserved arm has **0 women among 24 observed winners, plus ward 112 unresolved**; the previously nonreserved arm has **3 among 76**. The [typed panel](../../../karnataka/build/urban_expansion/ward_panel_2010_2015.parquet), dictionary, source-conflict ledger, and audit preserve these distinctions. This establishes a measured conditional comparison, not evidence that women's quotas were assigned by lottery.

Chandigarh's new [2016 official report](https://chandigarh.gov.in/files/GEMCC-2021/election-report-2016.pdf) explicitly retains the geography used in 2006 and 2011 (PDF pages 9–10), prints the quota histories and October allocation (pages 6 and 20), and supplies winner-linked Sh./Smt. titles in candidate nomination lists (pages 96–123). Its [typed panel](../../../chandigarh/release/ward_panel_2011_2016.parquet) has **26 wards and complete honorific-based outcomes**, with administrative recorded-sex fields left null. Honorific measurement is separately labeled. Detailed returns contain 122 candidates and 26 NOTA rows, with all ward totals reconciled; an inconsistent report summary is preserved in the [audit](../../../chandigarh/release/ward_panel_2011_2016_audit.json). No woman won any of the 17 later nonquota wards: 0/6 previously reserved and 0/11 previously nonreserved. Four controls had earlier quota exposure in 2006; restricting to the 2011 lottery risk pool leaves 0/6 versus 0/7. These are an additional cohort in the same city. The [design review](../../../chandigarh/source_review.md) documents the allocation reset, earlier history, and zero-event uncertainty.

## Rajasthan and Goa have concrete next steps

Rajasthan's [held 2009 report](../../../../../local_elections_rajasthan/data/raw/municipal/municipal_2009.pdf) and [2014 report](../../../../../local_elections_rajasthan/data/raw/municipal/municipal_2014.pdf) were already upstream. The new research recognizes their potential rather than claiming new downloads. The 2009 extracted file has 1,612 winners; the 2014 report describes 1,696 wards across 46 bodies and prints winner name, M/F, seat quota, personal category, age, and party on PDF pages 39–62. Original download URLs were not recorded, and the upstream provenance log says so. A contemporaneous [June 2014 delimitation lead](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/jaipur/govt-to-begin-delimitating-wards-today/articleshow/37536276.cms) identifies a state program covering 14 bodies, but does not prove the others unchanged. The priority is the original municipal delimitation notification or matched old/new boundary descriptions. No unchanged subset has yet been certified. Archived government catalogs were recovered; they did not resolve this gap. The [north/central ledger](../notes/north_central.md) records those bounded attempts.

Goa's newly recovered [2015 SEC gazette](https://sec.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Result-of-Gen-Elec-to-11-Mun-Councils-2015.pdf) has **159 declarations across 11 councils**. The [2021 official gazette delivered through a public mirror](https://lexcomply.com/rsjadmin/news/202105095809Notf%204-21-GMCNSE-SEC-2021-1136-%20Declare%20ward%20wise%20Election%20Results%20indicat%20vote%20polled%20each%20candidate.pdf) has **93 declarations**: Quepem 13, Sanguem 10, Margao 25, Mormugao 25, and Mapusa 20 (sections begin on PDF pages 1, 3, 5, 8, and 12). Both originals are retained in [Goa raw sources](../../../goa/raw/urban_expansion); these are inspected declaration counts, not released candidate tables. The 2021 document prints ward quotas; neither results document supplies an individual sex field. Quepem had 11 wards in 2015, and unchanged counts elsewhere do not establish unchanged boundaries. The July 7, 2020 delimitation notification remains missing.

Goa also requires the correct election-time reservation instrument. The retained [March 12, 2021 Supreme Court judgment](https://sec.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/judgement_supreme_court_dated_12-03-2021.pdf), PDF pages 5 and 96, documents the invalidation of the February reservation order for these councils. That invalidated order cannot supply April treatment assignments. The final roster, 2015 quotas, winner-sex evidence, and geography all precede estimation. The five towns share one state assignment system. [Inspection and acquisition details](../notes/east_west.md).

## More records do not always add the requested comparison

Tamil Nadu's newly collected [GO 124](https://www.tniuscbe.org/download/go/go1118.pdf), page 2, retains both boundaries and 2006 reservations in two corporations and 117 municipalities in 2011. These stable units therefore supply **no quota exits for that pair**. [GO 141](https://www.tniuscbe.org/download/go/go1124.pdf), page 3, adds actual 2011 reservation lists covering 355 wards in eight expanded municipalities; those require old quotas and geographic reconstruction. Earlier 2001→2006 histories remain a collection priority, not an established panel. Likewise, the held [Hyderabad release](../../../../../local_elections_telangana/data/release/urban/ghmc/README.md) documents no exits in 2016→2020, and the [Patna release](../../../../../local_elections_bihar/data/reference/urban/patna/README.md) records identical reservation categories across all 75 wards in 2017 and 2022. Longer reservation durations require a distinct treatment-history comparison.

The newly obtained [Turnbull supplement](https://static.cambridge.org/content/id/urn:cambridge.org:id:article:S1743923X19000722/resource/name/S1743923X19000722sup001.docx) contains 11 summary tables, reports 3,784 candidates, and covers Kanpur, Lucknow, Allahabad, and Varanasi in 2012→2017. It overlaps held UP evidence; it is not a microdata deposit or four new studies. Candidate entry, nomination, and recontesting are useful separate outcomes, but the supplement does not establish raw sex measurement, person-linkage rules, or ward continuity. The older [ISS–CSH four-city project](https://www.academia.edu/11667146/Gender_in_municipal_elections_Delhi_Mumbai_Chennai_Kolkata_) points to Kolkata 2000 and Chennai 2001, but no underlying microdata were recovered.

The remaining gaps are specific. Kerala has held geographic evidence but unresolved election-dated sex measurement; later council rosters include replacements. Odisha already has 294 winner records and a 55-document affidavit pilot, but no completed winner-sex extraction or historical geographic bridge. All three pages of Kolkata's held 2015 winner gazette were visually checked: they have names and wards, without a sex field or systematic sex-specific honorifics. New Haryana reports add actual municipal returns, but inspected individual winner rows lack sex; Punjab's acquired candidate pages are partial. Gujarat and other Maharashtra multi-member wards require a different exposure definition, and no usable mayor/chairperson series was recovered. Rajasthan's chairperson outcomes also involve a direct-to-indirect election change. These are substantive measurement or design gaps, distinct from the logged access failures for particular WB, Jharkhand, Assam, and MP endpoints. Other Northeast states, Andhra Pradesh, Puducherry, and several northern states were not exhausted. [Regional ledgers: north/central](../notes/north_central.md), [east/west](../notes/east_west.md), and [south](../notes/south.md) distinguish inspected sources from located leads.

## Remaining collection priorities and synthesis scope

Bengaluru and the additional Chandigarh cycle now have checked data handoffs. The highest-value new acquisition is Rajasthan's 2014 municipal delimitation evidence; Goa follows because it still needs several independent source components. For the rural–urban comparison, repeated Chandigarh cycles and multiple municipalities under a common state system require dependence-aware pooling. Candidate outcomes, municipal heads, multi-member wards, and longer reservation regimes should remain separately defined comparisons. Readiness and design determine inclusion.

The [combined inventory](combined_inventory.csv) preserves **46 source-inventory rows: 21 north/central, 14 east/west, and 11 south**. These are not 46 election pairs or independent studies. North/central rows describe sources or requests; east/west and south rows describe settings or pairs. Its primary key is `(inventory, source_row)`; `record_unit` makes this distinction explicit. Source and acquisition statuses are retained, validated panel statuses are updated, missing fields remain blank, and north/central's joint quota evidence is not split into invented prior/current fields. Paths are relative to the shared repository parent. `analysis_ready` carries the source inventories' declarations; blank means not supplied, not approval. The Chandigarh panel is now in its release directory; Bengaluru is a typed supplemental build with its own source audit. Neither is added to the rural pooled master.

Raw originals stay in their owning repositories with URL, retrieval-time, and hash receipts. Newly collected material is checked in the [south document audit](../notes/south_document_checks.json), [east/west receipt audit](../notes/east_west_receipt_audit.json), and [north/central request log](../north_central_requests.jsonl). Existing sources were inspected in place. This report records source validation and observed counts; effect estimation remains in the research manuscript pipeline. Updated November 9, 2026.
