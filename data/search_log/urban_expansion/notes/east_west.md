# Eastern and western urban election evidence

## Which new material can support an additional election pair?

### Takeaway
The strongest new acquisition is a Goa gazette containing candidate votes, ward reservations and 93 winner declarations across five smaller municipalities in 2021. It is not yet a persistence dataset: the 2015 result gazette has now been recovered with 159 declarations, but individual winner sex and prior quotas remain unavailable, and geographic continuity is not established. No pair in this handoff is marked analysis-ready.

### Cited Findings
- The April 28,2021 official gazette declares elections held April 23 for Quepem, Sanguem, Margao, Mormugao and Mapusa. Its 15 pages contain 93 declarations:13,10,25,25,20 respectively. Candidate votes and ward quota labels are printed together. Sections begin on PDF pages 1,3,5,8,12. **Official document, mirror transport:** [retained Lexcomply PDF](https://lexcomply.com/rsjadmin/news/202105095809Notf%204-21-GMCNSE-SEC-2021-1136-%20Declare%20ward%20wise%20Election%20Results%20indicat%20vote%20polled%20each%20candidate.pdf). Local: `local_elections/data/goa/raw/urban_expansion/municipal_results_2021_gazette_mirror.pdf`, SHA 256 `a9b1fbde766970737cb8360c2f884bb9e93ce40e6a92790a7af90bd1c5f47b7e`. Counts are inspected declaration counts, not a newly released parsed candidate table.
- **2015 original now recovered through Wayback:** the October 30, 2015 gazette declares the October 25 elections in 11 councils. It has 159 winner declarations across 27 PDF pages: Pernem 10, Mapusa 20, Bicholim 12, Valpoi 10, Margao 25, Cuncolim 12, Quepem 11, Curchorem-Cacora 14, Sanguem 10, Mormugao 25, Canacona 10. [Original SEC URL](https://sec.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Result-of-Gen-Elec-to-11-Mun-Councils-2015.pdf), recovered capture `20220702194214`. Local: `local_elections/data/goa/raw/urban_expansion/municipal_results_2015_archived.pdf`, SHA256 `53d9b81374713dcbea6e9361b9d680aa8d914955bffa6bdb62a95abecfa30036`. Candidate votes are printed; no explicit sex field or quota labels were found in these result tables. The [2015 SEC catalog](https://sec.goa.gov.in/municipal-council-2015/) separately lists the missing reservation roster.
- Comparing the inspected result documents, Quepem grows from 11 wards in 2015 (PDF page 15) to 13 in 2021 (PDF page 1 onward). Mapusa, Margao, Mormugao and Sanguem retain their counts, but that is not proof of stable boundaries. This is a concrete obstacle to a blanket ward-number join. Sources: [2015 SEC gazette](https://sec.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Result-of-Gen-Elec-to-11-Mun-Councils-2015.pdf); [2021 official gazette mirror](https://lexcomply.com/rsjadmin/news/202105095809Notf%204-21-GMCNSE-SEC-2021-1136-%20Declare%20ward%20wise%20Election%20Results%20indicat%20vote%20polled%20each%20candidate.pdf).

- The August 17,2020 official notice states that these 11 councils first met November 5,2015, and describes a July 7,2020 delimitation notification. Therefore equal ward counts alone cannot authorize a 2015→2021 join. This notice was inspected through indexed official text; local request 403: [notice, printed page 552](https://goaprintingpress.gov.in/downloads/2021/2021-20-SII-EOG-1.pdf).
- **Reservation judgment now recovered:** [State of Goa v Fouziya Imtiaz Shaikh, March 12, 2021](https://sec.goa.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/judgement_supreme_court_dated_12-03-2021.pdf), archived capture `20240902110904`, is held at `local_elections/data/goa/raw/urban_expansion/reservation_judgment_2021_archived.pdf`. It is a 96-page image PDF. Targeted Tesseract English OCR inspected PDF pages 2–15 and 94–96: page 5 reproduces the High Court order quashing the February 4, 2021 reservation order for the same five councils and requiring a fresh order; pages 10–12 describe the parties' arguments about rotation. These arguments are not themselves findings that any ward boundary is unchanged. Do not adopt the invalidated February quota order as the April election treatment. PDF page 96 confirms that the February 4 reservation order was set aside and that a fresh election schedule was required. The corrected final roster and detailed primary geography remain required.


### Inferences
- Prioritize these five Goa towns together. They share one state assignment system; additional municipalities are useful observations, not automatically independent studies.
- A complete source extraction must distinguish seat reservation from candidate sex. This pass does not infer sex from names or women-only offices. Nomination records/affidavits may supply an independent outcome or explicit source honorific, but none is yet verified.

### Gaps
2015 reservation file; election-time sex for both waves; revised 2021 quota order; municipal-boundary continuity or a crosswalk. Result declarations alone do not resolve these. Both result gazettes have been read as text but have not been promoted to a checked Parquet release.

## What do the additional academic sources contribute?

### Takeaway
Turnbull supplies a useful candidate/nomination/recontesting comparison, but its four cities are already-covered Uttar Pradesh cities. The older ISS–CSH four-city project may supply historical Kolkata and Chennai information; no public microdata has been verified.

### Cited Findings
- Brian Turnbull’s *Quotas as Opportunities and Obstacles* is [DOI 10.1017/S 1743923 X 19000722](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1743923X19000722). The recovered [publisher DOCX supplement](https://static.cambridge.org/content/id/urn:cambridge.org:id:article:S1743923X19000722/resource/name/S1743923X19000722sup001.docx) explicitly names Kanpur, Lucknow, Allahabad and Varanasi. It tabulates 2017 candidate gender/community and nominations, and 2012→2017 incumbent return. It reports 3784 candidates. Local: `local_elections/data/reference/urban_expansion/academic/turnbull_2019_online_appendix.docx`. This is 11 summary tables, not a microdata deposit.
- The [author research page](https://brianturnbull.weebly.com/research.html), retained locally, exposes no relevant election dataset in its Data and Appendices section; the download there concerns a different article. The [USF repository entry](https://digitalcommons.usf.edu/soc_facpub_sm/4/), also retained, links back to the DOI. The publisher PDF URL returned HTML access content, not a PDF. Title/author/dissertation searches did not identify an accessible microdata deposit. This is a bounded negative search, not proof that no deposit exists.
- Stéphanie Tawa Lama-Rewal’s author-uploaded [*Gender in municipal elections*](https://www.academia.edu/11667146/Gender_in_municipal_elections_Delhi_Mumbai_Chennai_Kolkata_) identifies Kolkata 2000, Chennai 2001, and Mumbai/Delhi 2002. The [related author-uploaded chapter](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/283720563_Gender_in_municipal_politics_Delhi_Mumbai_Chennai_Kolkata) identifies the 2005 Ghosh–Tawa Lama-Rewal volume. Web text inspected; direct Academia download/page request 403. No source microdata recovered.

### Inferences
- Turnbull is not four new independent urban settings beyond our UP sample. Candidate entry, party nomination, incumbent return, and winning after quota withdrawal are distinct outcomes. Do not substitute one for another or count overlapping source data twice.
- The appendix alone does not document whether candidate sex was read from an administrative field or how former winners were linked to later candidate names. Those measurement questions remain open; matching names is not geographic validation.

### Gaps
Full Turnbull microdata/code, exact candidate-sex construction, name-linkage rules and boundary handling; public ISS–CSH election/survey data; author dissertation access. No authors were contacted and no paid databases were used.

## What other regional sources were inspected, and what remains blocked?

### Takeaway
The new Bihar and Gujarat originals clarify coverage and design but do not add ward-level persistence observations. WB, Jharkhand and Assam access problems are recorded precisely. Previously collected Odisha files were not duplicated.

### Cited Findings
- The recovered [RCUES Bihar 2007 table](https://rcueslucknow.org/states/Compendium%20of%20Urban%20Data%20%28Bihar%29/_46_%20WOMEN%20REPRESENTATION%20IN%20ULB%20OF%20BIHAR.pdf), PDFpage 1, reports 121 ULBs,2960 member seats and 1462 women members, by ULB tier. It does not identify individual wards, winners or quota histories. It also prints 3 female heads among 7 corporations alongside 48.86%, an internal inconsistency; raw values are preserved. Local: `local_elections_bihar/data/raw/urban_expansion/urban_women_2007_rcues.pdf`. Do not use its aggregate sex totals as individual outcomes.
- The recovered [February 24,2021 Supreme Court judgment](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2015/28253/28253_2015_37_1501_26431_Judgement_24-Feb-2021.pdf), PDFpages 13 and 54, describes Gujarat’s 2015 rule reserving two seats in each ward for women. Local: `local_elections/data/gujarat/raw/urban_expansion/municipal_reservation_judgment_2021.pdf`. A multi-member ward exposure cannot be equated with the single-seat quota-exit comparison. Chairperson/mayor rotation could be a separate design, but no office-level election/reservation series was recovered here.
- WB’s [historical municipal selector](https://archive.wbsec.org/Municipal/Detailed_Mun_Yearwise.aspx) is a potentially broader source than Kolkata. The held order catalog supplies exact [Bidhannagar 2015 reservation](https://archive.wbsec.org/writereaddata/Orders/Municipal/FORM%20-%20B-Bidhannagar.pdf) and [Asansol 2015 delimitation](https://archive.wbsec.org/writereaddata/Orders/Municipal/Delimitation-Asansol%20MC-2015.pdf) links. All three new live requests returned 503. Three serialized exact-URL wayback-package searches returned no captures; these do not rule out other host/protocol/encoding variants. Logs: `local_elections/data/west_bengal/raw/urban_expansion/`.
- [Jharkhand’s municipal selector](https://sec.jharkhand.gov.in/MElection.aspx) is located, but Python retrieval failed certificate validation; an HTTP attempt timed out. TLS was not disabled. [Assam’s Finance Commission memorandum](https://www.sfcassam.nic.in/14thFC/14thFC-Memorandum.pdf), section 11.62, identifies the 2013 GMC election; no individual returns were acquired. [A 2013 boundary notification is identified in later litigation](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/140892846/), so the Guwahati historical bridge requires explicit checking rather than repeated ward labels.
- The previously located [Pune 2017 candidate-vote file](https://www.pmc.gov.in/sites/default/files/Election_2017_Candidate_Voting_Count.pdf) returned 404. No new Pune/Pimpri/Nagpur returns were acquired. Their multi-member offices and any mayor/chairperson series need separate estimands and source collection.
- Odisha’s [2022 report](https://sec.odisha.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/ULBs_Final-2022-12-10-2023-FINAL_compressed.pdf) and 2013/14 report are already held. Existing 294 winner records and the 55-document affidavit pilot are documented in `local_elections_odisha/data/release/urban/README.md`; this pass adds no sex measurement or geographic linkage and makes no duplicate copies.

### Inferences
Bihar’s two-election treatment histories, Gujarat/Maharashtra multi-member councils, and indirectly selected municipal heads belong in explicitly separate comparisons. A common election system, common original administrative source, and repeated city observations induce dependence; town count is not study count.

### Gaps
No new analysis-ready pair is asserted. Bihar 2007→2012 and 2012→2017 ward microdata remain unverified; WB historical candidate-sex and primary quotas remain incomplete; Ranchi 2008/2013/2018 records were not recovered; Guwahati 2003/2013/2022 geography and recorded sex remain unresolved. Other Northeast states and Gujarat/Maharashtra municipal-head histories were not substantively advanced in this bounded pass. Source URLs, successful payload hashes, and failed requests are in each owner’s new `urban_expansion/receipts.jsonl`; no existing releases, manuscript, or central catalog was edited.

The second, bounded Wayback pass recovered the exact 2015 result and court-judgment URLs above. The 2020 delimitation-history URL again returned no captures. All archive access was serialized with the other research agents; the window is released. The archive receipt distinguishes capture time from retrieval time. The actual July 7, 2020 boundary notification has not been recovered. The 2010→2015 Goa pair remains unassessed: no 2010 result/quota files or geographic bridge were recovered in this pass.

Kolkata gazette verification: all three pages of the held April 29, 2015 gazette were visually inspected (printed pages 295–297). The tables have only serial number, member name, and ward number. They contain no sex column or systematic Shri/Smt/Mr/Ms honorifics. A doctor title is not sex-specific; Begum and similar components occurring within names were not interpreted as independently recorded sex. This confirms a measured source gap rather than leaving the gazette unread. Original source provenance remains in `local_elections/data/west_bengal/raw/kolkata/reference/manifest.csv`; no duplicate PDF was made.
