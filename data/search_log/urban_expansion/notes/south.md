# Southern urban election evidence

Research completed November 9, 2026. This is an acquisition and source audit, not an estimated-effect comparison. The accompanying `south.csv` distinguishes acquired evidence from leads. Original bytes, URLs, acquisition times, and SHA-256 hashes are in each raw directory's `manifest.jsonl`; `south_document_checks.json` verifies all 14 newly retained originals. Existing OpenCity and state-repository files were read in place, not copied.

## Can Bengaluru support an additional 2010–2015 comparison?

### Takeaway

Yes. All 396 assignment records and all 198 original 2015 winner titles have been reviewed. The typed panel has 101 later nonquota wards: 25 previously reserved and 76 previously nonreserved. One outcome in that comparison remains unresolved. This is a validated conditional comparison; the held documents do not establish women's reservation by lottery.

### Cited Findings

- The archived Karnataka SEC supplied the 16-page 2010 winner gazette and 19-page 2015 winner gazette. They print ward number, ward name, reservation category, elected member's name and title, address, and party. All 16 pages of 2010 assignments and all 19 pages of 2015 winner titles were visually reviewed. These are winner records, not full candidate returns. Their names carry separately printed Shri/Shrimati titles; reservation was not used to supply sex. Local files: `local_elections/data/karnataka/raw/urban_expansion/bbmp_results_2010.pdf` and `bbmp_results_2015.pdf`. — [2010 original SEC gazette, archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20201117030355id_/https://karsec.gov.in/PreElectionHandler.ashx?id=63&Key=R); [2015 original SEC gazette, archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20201102050802id_/https://karsec.gov.in/PreElectionHandler.ashx?id=65&Key=R).
- The 2015 reservation PDF is not merely a withdrawn April draft. Physical page 1 is Gazette No. 738, June 23, implementing the June 20 order. It explicitly recounts the April 24 reversal, the May 5 Supreme Court restoration, withdraws the April 25 cancellation, and reinstates the April 13 roster. Physical page 2 contains the April 13 final notification, Gazette No. 327; point 3 refers ward boundaries to notification UDD 59 MLR 2009, July 15, 2009. Physical pages 3–7 contain all 198 reservation rows. The June 20 order does retain a qualification about pending boundary litigation. Local file: `local_elections/data/karnataka/raw/urban_expansion/bbmp_reservation_2015.pdf`. — [Archived SEC notification](https://web.archive.org/web/20150824072502id_/http://karsec.gov.in/BBMP-2015/reservation.pdf).
- The original July 15, 2009 boundary notification is now downloaded from Praja's mirror. Physical page 1 (printed page 70) names the same notification and specifies 198 single-member wards; its annexure gives localities and north/east/south/west boundary descriptions. This is actual geographic evidence, rather than a match on ward count. Local file: `local_elections/data/karnataka/raw/urban_expansion/bbmp_delimitation_2009.pdf` (99 pages). — [2009 notification and boundary schedules](https://praja.in/files/BBMPwards.pdf).
- The May 5 Supreme Court judgment, paragraphs 3 and 8, records the existing-boundary direction and restores the single-judge order. The later June 22 High Court decision's indexed paragraphs 36–40 expressly explain that the existing-boundary direction survived unchanged and reject reopening it. The Supreme Court text was inspected through web retrieval. Direct downloads of these court mirrors returned 403; the June 22 full page could not be retrieved and its status is therefore **located/indexed**, not a retained original. — [Supreme Court, May 5](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/103076892/); [High Court, June 22, indexed detailed text](https://sooperkanoon.com/case/amp/1195582/mr-c-k-rama-murthy-vs-state-election-commission).
- All five reservation-table pages were visually read. They identify **97 women-reserved wards**, not the 98 in OpenCity's “Final reservation” column. The sole women-flag discrepancy is ward 4: the original says Backward Category B without women; OpenCity adds women. The August 29 winner gazette, page 1, independently prints the non-women category for ward 4. The visual transcription is `local_elections/data/karnataka/manual/urban_expansion/bbmp_reservations_review.csv`: 396 year–ward rows across 2010 and 2015, string ward IDs, year, body, women-reservation flag, page and source hash. The second review also transcribed every caste category; the typed panel retains originals and secondary-source discrepancies. — [Original reservation pages 3–7](https://web.archive.org/web/20150824072502id_/http://karsec.gov.in/BBMP-2015/reservation.pdf); [OpenCity paired-reservation CSV](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/e32c1fea-1a57-43f0-a9ef-d01f53581d86/resource/d8a9a8a6-822c-437a-a689-7fe82e7c9c62/download/30af8888-d6d4-4189-b080-5467c53e9052.csv).
- Quota exits exist: ward 198 is BC-A women in the 2010 gazette, physical page 16, and General without women in the 2015 reservation and winner gazettes, physical pages 7 and 19 respectively. Ward 5 is non-women-reserved in both winner gazettes, physical page 1. This establishes variation, not assignment validity or an effect. Ward 189 is reserved in 2015, so the correction leaves the later-open frame unchanged. — [2010 original](https://web.archive.org/web/20201117030355id_/https://karsec.gov.in/PreElectionHandler.ashx?id=63&Key=R); [2015 original](https://web.archive.org/web/20201102050802id_/https://karsec.gov.in/PreElectionHandler.ashx?id=65&Key=R); [paired secondary roster](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/e32c1fea-1a57-43f0-a9ef-d01f53581d86/resource/d8a9a8a6-822c-437a-a689-7fe82e7c9c62/download/30af8888-d6d4-4189-b080-5467c53e9052.csv).

### Inferences

- The documents strongly support an exact 2010→2015 ward comparison. Geographic linkage can be established by the retained 2009 boundary schedule plus the 2015 implementing notification and judicial record, rather than name matching.
- The primary winner gazettes are enough to build an auditable title-based outcome source, but title errors must remain visible. Do not silently treat this measurement as an explicit administrative sex field.
- The new source audit increases confidence that this pair is feasible; it does not establish that quota assignment is random. The assignment rule and caste/population sorting must be examined separately before causal estimation.
- Additional Karnataka towns would share state rules and election administration. Their observations should not automatically become independent study-level policy experiments.

### Validated handoff and remaining limits

- The [typed panel](../../../karnataka/build/urban_expansion/ward_panel_2010_2015.parquet) has reviewed 2010/2015 quotas, caste categories, original 2015 titles, secondary explicit sex, and reconciled outcomes. Its [README](../../../karnataka/build/urban_expansion/README.md), dictionary and audit state the row-level sources and unresolved discrepancies.
- Two original-title/secondary-sex conflicts remain missing: ward 112 (Shrimati versus M) and ward 197 (Shri versus F). Ward 197 is currently reserved and does not enter the later-open sample. Ward 132's Dr. title is not sex-specific; its explicit secondary M is corroborated by the same-person Shri Dr S Raju in the retained ward budget, PDF page 263. No later roster is transferred wholesale.
- Eligible outcomes are 0 women among 24 observed prior-quota winners, with ward 112 missing, versus 3 among 76 prior-nonquota winners. Assignment-source discrepancies in wards 4 and 5 can be examined separately. The full transition is 76 neither, 56 newly reserved, 25 quota exits and 41 reserved twice.
- The final June 22 court text remains indexed rather than a retained original. Existing-boundary evidence is supplied by the retained notification and boundary schedule.
- Complete candidate returns/affidavits remain necessary for education, participation and recontesting. Winner gazettes do not measure those outcomes. A 34-person reelected-candidate affidavit table is a selected sample.

## What do the new Tamil Nadu orders establish?

### Takeaway

The new official orders identify a large stable-boundary comparison that has **no quota exits**, and supply actual 2011 reservation lists for eight smaller municipalities whose boundaries changed. They sharpen which data would answer persistence, rather than merely increasing the number of cities.

### Cited Findings

- GO (Ms.) 124, September 7, 2011, physical page 2, paragraphs 7–8, says the existing ward strengths and territorial areas continue in the remaining two corporations and 117 municipalities, outside the 16 expanded urban bodies. It also retains the 2006 ward reservations in those unchanged units. Paragraph 10 accepts the proposal; the appended rule amendment permits fresh reservation when territorial limits change before two complete terms. The expanded corporations listed are Chennai, Coimbatore, Tiruchirappalli, Madurai, Tiruppur, Erode, Vellore and Thoothukudi; the remaining corporations are Salem and Tirunelveli. Retained original: `local_elections_tamil_nadu/data/raw/urban_expansion/go_124_2011.pdf`, 4 pages. — [Official GO124](https://www.tniuscbe.org/download/go/go1118.pdf).
- GO (Ms.) 141, September 15, 2011, physical page 3, provides actual ward-reservation lists for **Kancheepuram (51 wards), Villupuram (42), Namakkal (39), Karur (48), Hosur (45), Pudukottai (42), Nagercoil (52), and Tirupathur (36)**: 355 wards in total. It separately enumerates SC general, SC women, and general women wards; omitted wards are not women-reserved under this schedule. Physical page 1 explains expansion and the usual two-term/ten-year retention rule. Retained original: `local_elections_tamil_nadu/data/raw/urban_expansion/go_141_2011.pdf`, 3 pages. — [Official GO141](https://www.tniuscbe.org/download/go/go1124.pdf).
- Both originals are logged in the owning repository's required `data/raw/fetch_log.csv` and adjacent manifest. The prescribed `make check` passed using the existing Python environment and `PYTHONPATH=../local_elections_tamil_nadu/src`: lint and formatting passed, 1,422 raw-file hashes matched, and 32 tests passed. Existing unrelated data warnings remain reported by the repository verifier. — [Local acquisition receipts](../../../../../local_elections_tamil_nadu/data/raw/fetch_log.csv).

### Inferences

- The unchanged 2006→2011 municipal wards are not a quota-exit persistence comparison under these orders: the reservation was retained too. They may inform other estimands, but not the requested transition out of reservation.
- The eight expanded municipalities should not be discarded wholesale. Retaining old and new boundary schedules could establish an exact unchanged subset. Their 2011 reservation treatment is now observable; old reservation and geographic continuity are the next constraints.
- For a broad state extension, 2001→2006 may be more promising than 2006→2011 because of the ten-year cycle, but no usable 2001→2006 pair has been established here.

### Gaps

- The 2006 reservation order referenced in GO141 is GO87, September 7, 2006. It and the old/new boundary schedules remain to be collected.
- No new individual candidate or winner sex records were acquired for these eight municipalities.
- The held Chennai 2011→2022 files remain subject to the 2018 delimitation problem; equal ward numbers should not be joined without boundaries. An exact unchanged subset remains possible, but is not demonstrated by this pass.

## What remains for Kerala, Hyderabad, Andhra Pradesh and Puducherry?

### Takeaway

The main obstacles are specific: Kerala needs reliable election-dated sex measurement, Hyderabad needs an earlier reservation cycle, and other southern settings need actual paired records and boundary documentation. This pass concentrated on Bengaluru once primary originals became available.

### Cited Findings

- Kerala's held urban review already documents 304 candidate links for 2010→2015 in four corporations and 414 for 2015→2020 in five preexisting corporations. The 2015 SEC order, paragraph 16 (physical page 242), preserves earlier constituencies except specified new/expanded bodies; the held CAG report, paragraph 2.5.1 (physical page 7), says the planned 2020 delimitation was not conducted. The existing review also reports the 2020 candidate-PDF sex problem: among 172 linked comparisons with profiles, 100 show M versus F, 71 F versus M, and only one agrees. Later officeholder profiles can include replacements and cannot themselves be treated as election winners. The 2015 SEC report download timed out again in this pass; no false successful receipt was created. — [Owning repository review](../../../../../local_elections_kerala/data/search_log/sec/urban_review/README.md); [official 2015 report endpoint, inaccessible on this attempt](https://www.sec.kerala.gov.in/public/elercd/download/758ee616-0925-4aec-b5e3-002faa535e2c).
- Hyderabad's existing release already has official support that 2016 reservations were retained in 2020, with unchanged ward boundaries. It therefore lacks quota exits for that pair. Its 2020 return covers 149 wards, while complete profiles and the winner gazette cover the council; 2016 winner sex is still absent. These files were not duplicated. — [Owning GHMC release report](../../../../../local_elections_telangana/data/release/urban/ghmc/README.md).
- A 2019 study titled “Women's Representation in Urban Local Bodies: A Study of GHMC” is a possible route to 2009 records, but only its existence was located; the PDF was not downloaded or inspected during this bounded pass. It is not counted as acquired data. — [Located academic PDF](https://www.ijhsss.com/files/14.-Dr.-M.-Laxman-16-07-19.pdf).
- Puducherry's official local-administration history is a lead for distinguishing actual 2006 elections from later postponed processes. No second actual municipal election was established here, and neither 2011 nor 2021 notification dates were treated as observed election outcomes. — [Official history/delimitation page](https://lad.py.gov.in/node/1523); [SEC citizen charter](https://sec.py.gov.in/sites/default/files/sec-citizen-charter.pdf).

### Inferences

- A Kerala source-level correction would unlock more observations than another superficial city list. No global flip of the problematic sex field is justified by the current discrepancies.
- Hyderabad 2009→2016 requires original 2009 quota and outcome sources plus an actual boundary-retention or exact-crosswalk record; “150 wards in both years” is insufficient.
- Andhra Pradesh and other Karnataka councils remain inventory leads, not usable comparisons. The absence of a verified pair in this pass is not evidence that records do not exist.

### Gaps

- Kerala: original election-dated candidate or elected-member sex, and complete downloadable 2015 records.
- Hyderabad: 2009 SEC reservation/results originals and the 2009→2016 boundary/legal record.
- Andhra Pradesh: original 2014 and actual 2021 returns, reservation schedules, and boundary evidence; do not substitute the postponed 2020 timetable for an election.
- Other Karnataka councils: original 2013/2018 or other adjacent-cycle reports, seat design, quota rotations and boundary continuity.
- Puducherry: establish that a later municipal election actually occurred before searching for a persistence comparison.
