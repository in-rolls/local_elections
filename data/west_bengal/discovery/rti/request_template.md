# RTI request: Pradhan office reservation orders (Form 1B), West Bengal

One request per district, sent by post to the Public Information Officer of the
district's Panchayat Election Cell. Addresses are in `tracker.csv`.

Under West Bengal Right to Information Rules, 2006, the fee is Rs 10, paid by
Indian Postal Order or court-fee stamp. Copies cost Rs 2 per page, or Rs 50 per
CD. The applicant must be an Indian citizen. Send by Speed Post and keep the
receipt: the 30-day clock starts on the day the letter is received.

---

To
The Public Information Officer
Office of the District Magistrate and District Panchayat Election Officer
(Panchayat Election Cell / District Panchayat & Rural Development Office)
{DISTRICT} District, West Bengal
{ADDRESS}

**Subject: Application under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005**

Sir/Madam,

Please provide the following information under the Right to Information Act,
2005:

1. The final order in **Form 1B**, issued under **Rule 2A of the West Bengal
   Panchayat (Constitution) Rules, 1975**, that reserves the **offices of
   Pradhan** of Gram Panchayats in {DISTRICT} district for the Panchayat
   General Elections of **2008, 2013 and 2018**. For each order, include its
   memo number and date.
2. For the same three elections, the list of all Gram Panchayats in the
   district with the **serial numbers** assigned under Rule 2A(2)(a)(i) that
   were used to determine the reservation of Pradhan offices.
3. If the order for any of these years is not held in this office, the name
   and address of the office that holds it. Where appropriate, please transfer
   that part of this application under Section 6(3).

I would prefer copies in electronic form (PDF on CD, or by e-mail to {EMAIL}).
If they are not available electronically, please send certified photocopies.

The application fee of Rs 10 is enclosed as Indian Postal Order No. {IPO_NO}
dated {IPO_DATE}. I will pay any further copying charges on intimation.

I am a citizen of India.

Yours faithfully,

{NAME}
{POSTAL_ADDRESS}
{PHONE} · {EMAIL}
Date: {DATE}

---

## Why the request is worded this way

- **Form 1B and Rule 2A are named explicitly.** Every district uses these terms
  for the order (for example, the South 24 Parganas order No. 6/P&RD/PGE/2018).
  Naming them leaves no room to answer with ward-seat lists instead of
  Pradhan offices.
- **Item 2 asks for the serial numbers.** Some districts, such as Malda in
  2013, printed only the reserved GPs. Any GP not on the list is unreserved,
  but which GPs those are is known only from the full list. The serial numbers
  also let us rebuild the reservation roster independently as a check. They may
  also be what the `gpno` field in the Beaman et al. data refers to.
- **Item 3 invokes Section 6(3).** If a 2008 order has moved to archives or to
  the State Election Commission, the application is forwarded rather than
  refused.
