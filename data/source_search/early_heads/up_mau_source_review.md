The nine Mau PDFs are named Gram Panchayat reservation-history sources linked from the **GRAM PRADHAN** column of the retained [official district index](raw/up_mau_index.html). The index is titled “PANCHAYAT ELECTION 2021 : 1995-2015 RESERVATIONS DETAILS”; it distinguishes these links from BDC, Kshetra Panchayat Pramukh and Zila Panchayat material. The office label comes from that link context, not from a candidate’s name or sex.

All nine first pages, plus one middle and the last page of each PDF, were visually reviewed: **27 pages across 107 source PDF pages**. All 27 reviewed pages display columns for **1995, 2000, 2005, 2010, 2015 and 2021**. This confirms column presence, not complete nonmissing histories for every GP. The [source profile CSV](up_mau_source_profile.csv) retains each PDF’s full SHA256, official URL, reviewed page numbers, image hashes, page bounds, printed section headings and observed orientation. Originals and the existing Ghosi first-page image were preserved; no OCR or parser was run.

A printed row is a **GP appearance within a particular section**, with a historical code vector and population-related fields. Sections have headings such as SC, ST, OBC and L+UR and reorder overlapping GP lists. The same GP appears again with a different section serial. The parenthesized heading numbers are retained literally; their addends are not interpreted here. Population counts/percentages are demographic columns, not officeholders, ward counts or GP counts. Several sampled headers explicitly describe a 2011 census basis; the Badraon sampled headers do not print a census year. Ratanpura’s last L+UR page additionally prints an OBC-percentage column, so a parser must not assume one fixed demographic column layout.

**Missingness and repeated evidence matter.** Raw historical examples include `UR`, `SC`, `SC-L`, `ST`, `ST-L`, `OBC`, `OBC-L`, `L` and literal `NA`. These are examples, not an audited code dictionary. `NA` is visibly printed in many 1995–2010 cells and remains distinct from a blank or an unread cell. No expansion of `L` or a suffix, no women-reservation flag, and no winner-sex field is inferred in this review. No final/draft legal status is adjudicated.

The 2021 column is partly filled across the reviewed pages. Ranipur p1 and p8 have blank 2021 data cells, while p16 contains entries. In Ghosi, भवनपुर’s 2021 cell is blank in the SC list on p1 but reads `OBC` in the L+UR list on p6 and the OBC list on p11. Therefore a single-list blank must not become `UR`; a future extraction needs to retain each occurrence and reconcile repeated year-specific evidence explicitly. Ghosi p1’s 42 printed data rows are **not** a count of unique GPs in its eleven-page PDF. No document-level or district-level unique-GP total is estimated here.

Orientation is mixed even within a file: Ghosi p1 and p11 are upright, whereas p6 requires 270 degrees clockwise to become upright. The three reviewed Kopaganj pages also require 270 degrees clockwise; the other reviewed pages require 90 degrees clockwise. These observations apply only to the listed pages and must not become an unverified district-wide rotation rule. Some PDFs have substantial embedded native text, while Ghosi/Kopaganj first pages have none and Muhammadabad p1 has only a small text layer; native character counts in the CSV do not establish extraction accuracy.

Local validation checked nine source hashes against the acquisition log, all 27 image paths and hashes, page bounds/ranges, and CSV roundtrip. This is a bounded source-layout review, not full semantic parsing or deduplication. Existing acquisition logs and document queues were not changed.
