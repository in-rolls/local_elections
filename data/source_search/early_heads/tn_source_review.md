# Tamil Nadu early-head source review

This is a bounded source audit, not a parsed reservation dataset. Eight downloaded PDFs contain 1,444 PDF pages; 51 selected pages were visually inspected. The exact inspected pages, source SHA-256, rendered-image SHA-256 and paths are in [tn_review/page_review.csv](tn_review/page_review.csv). Original PDFs were read only and their hashes were rechecked after inspection. No OCR, pooled records, outcome joins or elected-person inference was performed. Metadata and native text extraction are retained in [tn_review/metadata.json](tn_review/metadata.json) and [tn_review/source_metadata.csv](tn_review/source_metadata.csv).

The strongest early village-president sources are Gazette No.401 of 1996, its 2001 amendments in No.636, the president portion of No.228 of 2006 with the errata in No.254, and No.318 of 2011. The reviewed roster tables explicitly include **General**, as well as caste and women reservations. They are not positive-reservation-only lists. Statewide completeness and exact statewide GP counts have **not** been established. None of the reviewed schedules contains elected president names or an elected-person sex field. Signatories are officials issuing the notification, not winners. General is a printed reservation category and does not establish that an elected president was male.

## Enumerated examples and identifiers

Only the following small source subsets were counted; these are not total source-record counts.

- **2001 Devakottai union replacement, pp2–3:** all printed serials 1–42 were visually enumerated. Rows1–4 are SC(Women); 5–10 SC(General); 11–20 General(Women); 21–42 General. Thus 42 named president entries, 10 caste-reserved and 14 women-reserved, with overlap of four. This is a complete replacement of that printed union section, not proof of statewide coverage.
- **2011 de-reservation amendment, pp1–2:** eight named changes were enumerated: Pulipakkam (Uthiramerur, Kancheepuram), Puthur (Chithamur, Kancheepuram), Ramapuram (Pallipet, Thiruvallur), Kolapakkam (Panruti, Cuddalore), Kadugapattu (Vallam, Villupuram), Seyyathuvannan (Thimiri, Vellore), Perayampattu (Thandarampet, Thiruvannamalai), Naduvalur (T.Palur, Perambalur). Each explicitly substitutes General for the specified former caste/category. This is not a claim that all other offices remained unchanged.

Village-president tables carry printed district and panchayat-union names, a local serial number, the village-panchayat name, and a reservation category. No stable statewide GP identifier was observed. Serial numbers reset across union sections and sometimes acquire insertions such as “4.A”; district renaming is itself part of the 2001 amendment. Some changes identify the old entry only by district, union and serial, requiring the referenced base notification. Preserve exact printed names and amendment operations; no automatic historical name crosswalk is justified here.

## Preservation and extraction constraints

- Six PDFs (1996, 2001 and 2006) yield no non-whitespace native text; `pdftotext` produces page separators only. The two 2011 PDFs have substantial English native text. Text availability is not a measure of extraction accuracy: tabular columns and Tamil legacy-font strings still require layout review.
- The 1996 No.401 scan has uneven contrast, clipped edges and overlays. PDFp110 is printedp108, PDFp113 is printedp111; the offset is therefore not constant across the file. PDFp114 is largely obscured/blank with a table-header fragment. No missing row was invented, and the location/cause of the earlier pagination offset was not audited.
- The 2006 No.228 scan similarly has a page offset: PDFp144 is printedp143, PDFp145 is printedp144, PDFp146 is printedp145. The first president table is PDFp2/printedp2, so do not derive all page locations using a constant offset. Section boundaries above were visually checked; intervening pages were not all inspected.
- The 2001 scan has page curvature, show-through and a scanner watermark. Full section reconstruction must apply changes to the 1996 source; copying every village name in its later ward descriptions would misclassify the tier and multiply office records.
- The 2006 errata changes actual reservation categories as well as names and headings. Examples visible on pp2–3 include swapping SC(Women)/SC(General) for named Kumaratchi entries, adding Women for Avalapalli, and changing several Andipatti categories. A parser of No.228 alone would therefore preserve superseded entries unless amendments are linked explicitly.
- PDF metadata creation dates refer to digitisation, not the historical notification date. Historical years and stages above were read from the printed notices. Metadata JSON retains the unmodified `pdfinfo` output for audit.
- Reviewed images are scope evidence at 90dpi, not final transcription crops. No claims of certified name-level accuracy, complete source enumeration, post-election officeholders or elected-person sex are made. The raw downloads remain the preservation artifacts.

Validation: eight source hashes rechecked against the originals; each of the 51 image-ledger entries resolves to a readable image and a valid source page; no source PDF was rewritten. Source counts, reviewed-page counts, and these illustrative row counts are deliberately distinct.
