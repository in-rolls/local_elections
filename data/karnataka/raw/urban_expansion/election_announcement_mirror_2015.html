BBMP General Election :

The Karnataka State Election Commission announces the dates for conduct of elections for 198 wards of BBMP, as contemplated under Rule 3 of Karnataka Municipal Corporation (Election) Rules 1979 and Section 55 of Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act 1976 as per reservation list published vide notifications

The Date on which a poll shall if necessary be taken 22-08-2015

ELECTION SCHEDULE :
1. Date and Day on which BBMP Commissioner will issue notification 03-08-2015 (Monday)
2. Last date for making Nominations. Date and Day 10–08–2015 (Monday)
3. Last date for Scrutiny of Nominations 11-08-2015 (Tuesday)
4. Last Date for withdrawal of Candidatures 13-08-2015 (Thursday)
5.  Timings of Poll 7.00 AM to 5.00 PM
6. The Date on which a re-poll shall if necessary be taken 24–08–2015 (Monday)
7. Counting of votes from 8.00 A.M. Onwards (Within 25-08-2015 BBMP Area) – (Tuesday)
8. Date and Day before which the process of election has to ||26-08-2015 be completed (Wednesday)

The Model Code of Conduct will come into force from dt:17-7–2015 and will be in force upto 26-08-2015, the date of completion of election process only in Bruhat Bangalore Mahanagara palike limits
