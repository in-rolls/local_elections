MINUTES OF THE ALL-POLITICAL PARTY MEETING HELD IN THE CONFERENCE HALL, DC OFFICE, SOUTH SALMARA MANKACHAR DISTRICT HATSINGIMARI AT 10.30 A.M ON 26/12/2024 REGARDING RESERVATION OF SEATS FOR SC/ST/WOMEN IN CONNECTION WITH ENSUING PANCHAYAT ELECTION 2024-25.

MEMBERS PRESENT: Annexure enclosed.

The meeting was presided over by Sri Rahul Kumar Gupta, IAS, District Commissioner, South Salmara Mankachar District, Hatsingimari. The District Commissioner welcomed all the members present in the meeting. He initiated the discussion and informed the house that as per direction of the Assam State Election Commission, the meeting has been convened for finalization of seats to be reserved for SC/ST and Women in respect of different levels of PRI constituency under South Salmara Mankachar Zilla Parishad. He further elaborated that as per Assam Panchayat (Amendment) Act.2011, the reservation of seats @50% of the total seats for all Panchayat bodies will be reserved for Women candidates including SC/ST. The chairman clearly informed the house that the reservation of seats are to be made on the basis of Census. 2001 population as per Notification issued vide No. E-586779/18 from the P & RD Deptt. Govt. of Assam.

As per population data of 2001 census 7 (Seven) Nos. of Ward, 1 (One) No. of G.P President and 1 (One) No. of G.P Vice President seats are to be reserved for the SC population. No reservation for ST category will be applicable in respect of South Salmara Mankachar District due to minimal number of populations of ST within the District as per 2001 census. However, considering the earlier precedence of 2018 Panchayat Election, the current ST population pattern and the unanimous consensus of the house, 2 (Two) numbers of Ward are reserved for the ST population. As agreed by the house the reservation of seats for Women has been made through the principle of rotation to the last Panchayat Election/2018, except ZP level. ZP level has been done through lottery.

Details of Reservation of Seats: -

(A) Reservation of seats for Zila Parishad Member Constituencies:

(Under Section 66 and Section 67 of the Assam Panchayat Act, 1994 and rule 8 of the Assam Panchayat (C) Rules 1995)

  • ➤ Total 4 number of ZP Member constituencies are there under South Salmara Mankachar Zilla Parishad. As per existing guideline 50% of total seats are to be kept reserved for Women. The house unanimously agreed for drawl of lottery proceeding. After the drawl of lottery the following 2 (Two) constituencies are kept reserved for Women candidates.
  • (1) 2- No. Kalapani ZPC
  • (2) 4- No Dakhin Salmara ZPC

(B) Reservation of seats for Anchalik Panchayat President:

  • ➤ South Salmara Mankachar District has 2 (Two) Nos. of Anchalik Panchayat viz (1) Mankachar A.P (2) Fekamari A.P
  • ➤ 1-Mankachar A.P kept reserved for women as per principal of rotation.

(C) Reservation of seats for Anchalik Panchayat Vice President:

  • ➤ Reservation for Women kept for Fekamari AP as per rotation principle.

(D) Reservation of seats for AP. Member:

As per the provision of the Assam Panchayat Act 1994 and Rule 7 of Assam Panchayat (Constitution) Rules, 1995 (as Amendment) reservation of AP Member should be made on the basis of population/areas within the Anchalik Panchayat. Accordingly, reservation of women in each AP shall not be less than 50% of the total seats.

  • ➤ Total No of AP = 2 Nos (covering 35 GPs)

All AP Member Constituencies are free from SC/ST reservation as per population pattern. The following 17 (Seventeen) Nos. AP Member constituencies are kept reserved for Women candidates as per principle of rotation and 1 (One) AP Member Constituency for women reservation has been selected randomly out of the previous election reserved constituencies for women as decided in the meeting.
