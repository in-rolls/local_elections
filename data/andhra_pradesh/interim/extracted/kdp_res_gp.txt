                              NOTIFICATION
             In pursuance of the orders issued by the Government vide G.O.Ms.No.559
PR&RD(E&R)       Dated:02.03.2020    and    G.O.Ms.No.560   PR&RD(E&R)       Dated:

Panchayats reserved for Scheduled Tribes, Scheduled Castes, Backward Classes, Un-
Reserved and Women (done by the Revenue Divisional Officers, Kadapa, Rajampeta
and Jammalamadugu) and also notifies the names of wards reserved for Scheduled
Tribes, Scheduled Castes, Backward Classes, Un-Reserved and Women(done by the
Mandal Parishad Development Officers concerned) in YSR District as shown in the
proforma appended to this Notification.
