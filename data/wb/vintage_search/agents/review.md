# Review of external-agent findings

Claude and agy were run on public-web-only research briefs outside this repository. Their reports are leads, not validated observations.

- Claude correctly identified the full Beaman package and its per-file metadata. The latest metadata has anonymous GP/block identifiers. The separate publicly posted in-rolls/beaman reservation DTA acquired by the main researcher has names; the report’s statement about anonymity does not apply to that older mirror.
- Claude suggested recovering identities from category patterns and block sizes. This was not used: those are not unique linkage evidence.
- Dataverse file HTTP 400 responses explicitly require guestbook 269. They are not evidence that the files are restricted or nonexistent. User-provided guestbook details are pending.
- The agy reports’ explanation of its final cancellation as a ten-minute cutoff is incorrect. The stream records a denied read_url action after about 48 seconds. Its search service also returned capacity errors.
- The first agy report conflates 89 villages with 57 GPs and gives an incorrect openICPSR identifier; the verified AER project is E112367V1.
- Neither agent’s inability to retrieve a roster establishes that it does not exist. No external-agent claim independently fills the coverage matrix.
- The Zenodo QE 2024 package (10805145) was subsequently downloaded and its two main data files inspected locally. The reservation variable found is for Assembly constituencies, not GP Pradhans. The package is retained as a checked lead and contributes zero GP reservation observations.

- The agy Redivis URL was not verified and must not be treated as an established dataset endpoint. Its claims of a fully verified older baseline are superseded by the local contradiction and cross-source audit.

- The final agy vintage ledger gives HTML line numbers as if they were judgment paragraph numbers. Use the saved HTML anchors and printed judgment pages instead. The cited Prabhati Sarkar judgment does not state a 12 February 2008 reservation-order date or a Basirhat-I block name; both were omitted from the curated observations. Its broad claims about all 1998–2007 judgments are not established by the inspected cases. Two named office-category statements were independently verified, with 2008 cycle linkage explicitly marked as inferred.
