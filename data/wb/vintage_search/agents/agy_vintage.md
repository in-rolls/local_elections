### Key Takeaways from the Evidence Ledger

1. **Known Anchor Verification (*Bidhya Charan Sinha*, [Indian Kanoon doc 1091911](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/1091911/))**:
   - **Order Dates / Terms**: Pertains to the **6th Panchayat General Elections (2003)** (Nomination: 11 April 2003; P&RD Circular: 9 June 2003; Poll: 17 June 2003; Pradhan election meeting: 28 June 2003; Paras 19, 33).
   - **Related Petitions**: Heard alongside *Mala Majhi v. State of W.B.* (W.P. 8805(W)/2003) and *Smt. Nirmala Goreit v. State of W.B.* (W.P. 9274(W)/2003) (Paras 28, 34).
   - **Office Category**: The office of Pradhan was reserved for **Scheduled Caste (SC)** (Para 1).
   - **Gram Panchayat Identification**: Verified negative. As suspected, **the judgment text does not disclose the Gram Panchayat name, block, or district** for any of the three petitioners. Per instructions, these remain strictly classified as unnamed records and are not counted.
