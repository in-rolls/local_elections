# Evidence Ledger & Research Plan: Gram Panchayat Pradhan Office Reservations (West Bengal 1998 & 2003)

## 1. Goal Description
The objective is to identify actual **named Gram Panchayat Pradhan office reservation statuses** in West Bengal for the **1998 (5th)** and **2003 (6th)** Panchayat General Elections, focusing specifically on district reservation orders quoted in Calcutta High Court judgments (1998–2007) across the 16 ordinary districts outside Birbhum:
- Bardhaman / Burdwan, Hooghly, Howrah, Nadia
- Bankura, Purulia, Malda, Murshidabad
- North 24 Parganas, South 24 Parganas
- Cooch Behar, Jalpaiguri, Uttar Dinajpur, Dakshin Dinajpur
- Purba Medinipur, Paschim Medinipur

Specific requirement: Identify verified records with **Gram Panchayat name**, **District / Block**, **Election term or order date**, **Exact office category (SC / ST / Women / UR)**, and **Primary citation with paragraph locator**. Unnamed records, ward reservations, petitioner caste assertions, and post-2007 elections (such as the 2008 7th general election) must not be misattributed.

## 3. Findings & Legal Analysis of 1998–2007 Judicial Repositories

1. **Absence of District Roster Schedules in Reported Judgments**:
   - In Calcutta High Court writ petitions from 1998–2007, judges consistently resolved constitutional questions in the abstract (e.g., whether an SC candidate from an unreserved ward can occupy a reserved Pradhan chair; whether Article 243D(4) overrides majority rule) without appending the underlying district Form 1B / Form 1A gazetted schedules.
   - Even in leading cases such as *Bidhya Charan Sinha* (AIR 2004 Cal 27), the High Court quoted the operational guidelines of the 9 June 2003 Circular verbatim (Para 19), but referred to the underlying panchayat only as "such Gram Panchayat reserved for Scheduled Caste candidate".

2. **The 2003 Legal Mechanism**:
   - The 6th Panchayat Elections (2003) were conducted under the *West Bengal Panchayat Act, 1973* and the *West Bengal Panchayat (Constitution) Rules, 1975* (specifically Rule 2A, Rule 3(7)(a)).
   - Pradhan office reservations were designated block-wise by the District Panchayat Election Officer (District Magistrate) via Form 1B (following Form 1A draft publication). These orders were published locally at the Block Development Office and the District Collectorate / Zilla Parishad, but were not reproduced in full within appellate court law reports.

## 4. Next Steps & Recommended Search Vectors

1. **State Gazette & District Administration Archives**:
   - Search the *Kolkata Gazette Extraordinary* (1998 and 2003 volumes), where District Panchayat Election Officers published statutory Form 1B notifications.
2. **West Bengal State Election Commission (WBSEC) Historical Archive**:
   - Examine archival reports of the 5th (1998) and 6th (2003) Panchayat General Elections published by WBSEC or the Department of Panchayats & Rural Development (P&RD).
3. **District Portal Wayback Crawls**:
   - Target official district portals (e.g., `bardhaman.nic.in`, `north24parganas.gov.in`, `medinipur.gov.in`, `purulia.gov.in`, `bankura.gov.in`) for archived Panchayat election / roster directories from the 2003 cycle.
